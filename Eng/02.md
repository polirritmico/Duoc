## Activity

Write about a memorable day.

> I remember one day a few years ago. It was around mid February. With my band
> we had three performances in 3 different cities. Each one has its own story,
> but long story short, we played at a festival in a location called El Manzano
> around 4am. Was a huge success, everybody was dancing and having a good time.
> The event was massive, like thousand of peoples, and the sound crew and
> equipment was amazing.

> Then we have to play on a school in Santiago at around 11am, so we didn't
> sleep at all that night, and traveled to Santiago on the first bus we could
> take. All this was carrying our music instruments/equipment on our shoulders.
>
> Finally, at night we had to play on a birthday party from one of our closest
> friends, but we were so tired that no one wanted it. But we go anyway, that
> was an extra bus travel to the beach... When we got there, the party was also
> incredible. And in the end, this friend heard about our travels, and since it
> was its last day on Chile, He collected with the rest of the guests money and
> gifts for us! He even gave us all his remaining pesos!
