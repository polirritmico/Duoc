# Class 2025-10-06. Grammar

Prepositions at, in, on, to.

## Time

- We use `on` for days on the weekend
- We use `at` for times of the day and night

- We also use `in` with months and years
- we also use `on` with dates

### In:

- in the morning
- in the afternoon
- in the summer
- in December
- in 2018

### on

- Monday
- January 1
- the weekend

### at

- three o'clock
- noon midnight
- lunchtime
- night

## Movement and place

- He goes `to` work `at` 8:00
- He has lunch `at` work.
- He works `in` an office

- We us `to` for movement or direction
- We use `at` and `in` for position
- We use: `at` work, `at` home, `at` school
- We use: `in` an apartment, `in` an office, `in` a room.
