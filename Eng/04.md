# Class 2025-10-11

## Adverbs

- Always
- Usually
- Often
- Sometimes
- Hardly-ever
- Never

## Usage

- I `usually` get up at 9 am.
- You `never` take a shower in morning.
- She
- He go`es` to work `everyday`.
- It
- We
- They `always` go to DUOC.
- `Do you always` wake up at 5 am?
- No, I `usually` wake up at 6 am.
- Yes, I `always` wake up at 5 am. `Then`, I have breakfast.
- Yes, I wake up at 5 `once a week`, because I work.
